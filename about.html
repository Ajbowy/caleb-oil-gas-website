About Us

Powering Homes and Businesses, Responsibly.

Caleb Oil/Gas LTD has grown into a dependable name in fuel, cooking gas and lubricant distribution, built on safety, integrity and a long-term commitment to our communities.

Our Story

Who We Are

Caleb Oil/Gas LTD was founded to bridge the gap between reliable energy supply and everyday household and business needs. What started as a single fuel outlet has grown into a full distribution network covering petrol, diesel, cooking gas and lubricants.

Today, we serve individuals, families and companies who depend on us for consistent, safe and honest service every single day.
